RetroBoot bitmap fonts

  RetroBoot System 12  (src/rb-system-12.glyphs  -> RetroBoot-System-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Regular 12 (src/rb-regular-12.glyphs -> RetroBoot-Regular-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Small 10   (src/rb-small-10.glyphs   -> RetroBoot-Small-10.bdf)

Provenance: original pixel art drawn from scratch for RetroBoot in October
2026, "in the spirit of" the classic Mac OS system fonts. No glyph was traced
or copied from Apple's Chicago, Charcoal, Geneva or Monaco, from the
M5Tab-Macintosh charcoal12.bdf, or from any other font. The .glyphs files are
the editable source; tools/fontgen.py generates the BDF files and
src/fonts_data.c from them. Covers printable ASCII, Latin-1 (U+00A0-U+00FF),
U+0131, dashes, curly quotes, bullet, ellipsis, euro, trademark, arrows,
the Command/Option/Shift key symbols and U+FFFD.
