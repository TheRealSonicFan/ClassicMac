RetroBoot bitmap fonts

  RetroBoot System 12  (src/rb-system-12.glyphs  -> RetroBoot-System-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Regular 12 (src/rb-regular-12.glyphs -> RetroBoot-Regular-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Small 10   (src/rb-small-10.glyphs   -> RetroBoot-Small-10.bdf)

Provenance: Regular 12, Small 10 and the System 12 extended glyphs are
original pixel art drawn for RetroBoot in October 2026. ClassicMac replaced
System 12's printable ASCII with the user's M5Tab-Macintosh Charcoal12 pixel
recreation (bcfa1649bcbd5a6c433ad4d09b93b543ae768275), traced from Mac OS 8
SimpleText screenshots. The source BDF records the reference hash. This is
not Apple's original font program. BDF files are the editable sources in
ClassicMac; rebuild embedded TrueType with build_platinum_swift.py.
