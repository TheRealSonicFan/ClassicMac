RetroBoot icons and cursor (assets/icons/*.icons -> src/icons_data.c)

Provenance: original pixel art drawn for RetroBoot in October 2026 in the
spirit of Mac OS 8/9 icons. No Apple icon bitmap was copied or traced. A few
icons take their starting layout from the user's own original drawings in
M5Tab-Macintosh (src/basilisk/preboot_ui.cpp: kMacSmall, kFolder, caution(),
stopSign(), note()); each such icon says so in a comment in its .icons file.
The Platinum control sprites in src/platinum.c are likewise ported from
M5Tab-Macintosh, where they were measured from Mac OS 8.1 on screen.
