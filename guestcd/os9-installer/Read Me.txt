ClassicMac Additions for Mac OS 9.2.1
=====================================

These additions were installed automatically by the regular Mac OS
installer.  No second installer pass is required.

System Folder additions
-----------------------

USB Overdrive provides native USB mouse scrolling and contextual clicks.
Its original registration information and documentation are in Apple
Extras:USB Overdrive.  Turn off ClassicMac's host-side Secondary Click and
Scrolling helpers when USB Overdrive is active so events are not doubled.

StuffIt Engine and its PowerPlug are installed for DropStuff 5.5.

Apple Extras
------------

StuffIt Expander 5.5, DropStuff 5.5, Transmit 1.6 PPC, and USB Overdrive
documentation are ready to use in the Apple Extras folder at the top level
of the startup disk.
